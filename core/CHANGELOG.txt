POSN Practice Judge - changes

4.0.0  Topics reorganised (your code moves with its problem, once; nothing you wrote is deleted):
       - English topic folders numbered in study order, basic to advanced: 01_Brute_Force_and_Implementation ...
         26_Bitmask_and_Digit_DP, in three groups: Core (01-14), Intermediate (15-25), Advanced (TOI) (26).
       - DP is now three core topics: 09 DP I Foundations, 10 DP II Classic Patterns, 11 DP III Faster Transitions
         (old topics 6 and 7, plus new problems); bitmask and digit DP moved to 26.
       - New intermediate topics: 16 Monotonic Stack and Queue, 18 Sparse Table, 19 Lazy Segment Tree (with the old
         lazy problems), 20 Tree DP, 21 Rerooting, 22 Euler Tour, 23 LCA and Binary Lifting, 24 SCC, Bridges and
         Articulation Points, 25 Trie.
       - Statements in the Camp 2 mock-exam layout (one or two pages, one sample per row).
       - An old Topic_K folder is removed only when nothing of yours is left in it; otherwise it gets README_MOVED.txt.
3.0.1  New statement layout: all text in Sarabun (a looped Thai font), more space between lines and below
       headings, sample labels inside the sample boxes; every statement still fits on one A4 page.
       New sets come with empty 1.cpp, 2.cpp, ... (your existing files are never changed); judging an empty file says so.
3.0  Two new topics: 14 number theory and counting, 15 strings and parsing (40 problems). Follows the POSN 2569
     Camp 2 syllabus: new problems in 7/7, 7/16, 8/3, 8/10, 9/8, 9/9, 9/20, 13/14 and 13/18 (the old ones used
     content outside the syllabus or repeated another problem); new solutions for 5/17 and 9/17 (tests unchanged).
     Topic 13 and the LCA problems of topic 12 are marked (เสริม): optional content of the syllabus.
     A formula in a statement never breaks across two lines.
2.1  A big test left over from an older version of a problem is rebuilt, so it is never judged with old data.

     6-7 DP, 8 graphs, 9 divide and conquer, 10 shortest paths, 11 DSU/MST, 12 trees, 13 segment/Fenwick trees.
