POSN Practice Judge - changes

4.0.0  The first release for students: 33 topics and 11 Mixed sets, one folder per problem.
       - English topic folders numbered in study order, basic to advanced: 01_Brute_Force_and_Implementation ...
         33_Mo_Algorithm, in three groups: Core (01-14), Intermediate (15-25), Advanced (TOI) (26-33).
       - DP is three core topics: 09 DP I Foundations, 10 DP II Classic Patterns, 11 DP III Faster Transitions;
         bitmask and digit DP are topic 26.
       - Intermediate topics: 15 Trees, 16 Monotonic Stack and Queue, 17 Fenwick and Segment Tree, 18 Sparse Table,
         19 Lazy Segment Tree, 20 Tree DP, 21 Rerooting, 22 Euler Tour, 23 LCA and Binary Lifting, 24 SCC, Bridges
         and Articulation Points, 25 Trie.
       - Advanced (TOI) topics, 12 problems each: 26 Bitmask and Digit DP, 27 DSU on Tree, 28 Heavy-Light
         Decomposition, 29 Centroid Decomposition, 30 Persistent Segment Tree, 31 Convex Hull, 32 Convex Hull Trick
         and DP Optimization, 33 Mo's Algorithm.
       - 11 Mixed sets (folders 00_Mixed/M01 ... 00_Mixed/M11, 6 problems each) with problems from different topics;
         the topic of a problem is not given.
       - ชุดฝึกรวมอยู่ในโฟลเดอร์ 00_Mixed (บนสุด)
       - เพิ่มชุดฝึกรวม M09-M11 · ปรับโจทย์ 11 ข้อ
       - Statements in the Camp 2 mock-exam layout (one or two pages, one sample per row). Problems are ordered
         from easier to harder within each level.
       - One folder per problem: 04_Binary_Search/05/ holds your code file 04-05.cpp (empty at first) and the
         problem's own statement 05_<Title>.pdf. A Mixed set: 00_Mixed/M01/3/M01-3.cpp and 3_<Title>.pdf. The
         whole topic is still in Problems_<topic>.pdf in the topic folder. Do not rename the folders or the .cpp
         files.
       - A problem folder holds exactly those two files: Makefile and judge.bat are in the topic folder (or the
         Mixed set folder, e.g. 00_Mixed/M01) only.
       - Judging: Ctrl+Shift+B in VS Code on the open .cpp; in a topic folder: make 5 for problem 5, make for the
         whole topic (Windows: judge 5, judge); in a problem folder: python3 ../../Judge/judge.py; anywhere:
         python3 <path>/Judge/judge.py FILE.cpp. Programs are compiled in Judge/.build/ (removed after the run),
         not in your folders.
       - Later updates: your written file moves with its problem; the file of a replaced problem is kept in
         <topic>/old/<n>_<OldTitle>.cpp. Nothing you wrote is deleted or overwritten.
3.0.1  New statement layout: all text in Sarabun (a looped Thai font), more space between lines and below
       headings, sample labels inside the sample boxes; every statement still fits on one A4 page.
       New sets come with empty 1.cpp, 2.cpp, ... (your existing files are never changed); judging an empty file says so.
3.0  Two new topics: 14 number theory and counting, 15 strings and parsing (40 problems). Follows the POSN 2569
     Camp 2 syllabus: new problems in 7/7, 7/16, 8/3, 8/10, 9/8, 9/9, 9/20, 13/14 and 13/18 (the old ones used
     content outside the syllabus or repeated another problem); new solutions for 5/17 and 9/17 (tests unchanged).
     Topic 13 and the LCA problems of topic 12 are marked (เสริม): optional content of the syllabus.
     A formula in a statement never breaks across two lines.
2.1  A big test left over from an older version of a problem is rebuilt, so it is never judged with old data.
2.0  13 topics in study order: 1 brute force, 2 prefix sums, 3 binary search, 4 greedy, 5 sweep line,
     6-7 DP, 8 graphs, 9 divide and conquer, 10 shortest paths, 11 DSU/MST, 12 trees, 13 segment/Fenwick trees.
1.1  Topics 12 (brute force) and 13 (divide and conquer); figures in the statements.
1.0  C2 Practice: 11 topics x 20 problems (easy to hard: 1-7 easy, 8-16 medium, 17-20 hard). Same judge as the
     Camp 1 practice and the Camp 2 mock exams; big tests are built on your computer the first time a problem is
     judged (small download). Memory limit 256 MB.
